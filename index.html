About

Engineering autonomy from the state-estimation layer.

Hi, I'm Ali. I am a software engineer specializing in multi-sensor fusion and localization for autonomous driving. Welcome to my portfolio, where I showcase my work building precise navigation systems using Modern C++, ROS2, and localization algorithms.

My work involves combining measurements from GNSS/INS, IMU, LiDAR and Cameras to estimate reliable vehicle motion and pose in challenging environments.

I am particularly interested in state estimation algorithms like Kalman filtering and factor-graph optimization with including LiDAR-Inertial Odometry(LIO) and Visual-Inertial Estimation.
